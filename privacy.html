Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Wait What If Dashboard is a private, locally operated social analytics dashboard used to display TikTok account statistics such as follower counts and public video view counts.

Information accessed

When a user authorizes TikTok access, the dashboard may access basic account information and account statistics permitted by the scopes granted through TikTok Login Kit, including follower count and public video information.

How information is used

Information is used only to display analytics on the local dashboard and to calculate local day-over-day metrics. The dashboard does not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Storage

TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the companion computer and are not intentionally stored on the ESP32/CYD display device. Local baseline metric data may also be stored on the companion computer.

Data sharing

No TikTok account data is intentionally shared with third parties by the dashboard, except as required to communicate with TikTok's official APIs.

Data retention and deletion

Local credentials and cached metric data can be removed by deleting the dashboard's local TikTok credential and state files or by revoking the app's access from the user's TikTok account settings.

Security

The dashboard is designed to keep TikTok credentials on the local companion computer and expose only sanitized metrics to the local display device.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the dashboard's functionality changes.
